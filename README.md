## 📄 Game Document

### Game Title
*SpellQuest* (in-game title screen: "Spell Caster") — Help the wizard battle monsters and rescue the princess by casting the right word!

### Target Learners
Beginner-level Thai-speaking English learners (suitable for lower-secondary school students or adult beginners / A1 level). The game assumes learners can already read basic English words but are still building vocabulary recognition speed and confidence — no prior gameplay instruction from a classmate or teacher is needed to play.

### Language Objective
*Skill focus:* Receptive vocabulary recognition — matching a Thai word to its correct English translation under light time pressure.

*Topic coverage:* 5 themed vocabulary sets (12 words each, drawn 6 at a time per playthrough):

- *Stage 1 – Nature:* tree, mountain, river, forest, bird, flower…
- *Stage 2 – Town & Community:* house, market, bridge, shop, street, tower…
- *Stage 3 – Spooky / Halloween:* ghost, bat, spider, skeleton, witch…
- *Stage 4 – Desert:* desert, camel, pyramid, sand, oasis, snake…
- *Stage 5 – Castle & Fantasy:* castle, sword, crown, dragon, princess, knight…

This lets a teacher assign the game to reinforce a specific thematic word list, or let students progress through all five as a general vocabulary review.

### How to Play
1. Click *START GAME*, then choose a stage from the map (later stages unlock as earlier ones are cleared).
2. A Thai word appears at the top of the screen with *two English answer buttons* below it.
3. A monster begins walking toward your wizard as a *time bar* counts down. Click the English word that correctly translates the Thai word *before the monster reaches you*.
4. *Correct answer:* the wizard casts a spell that damages the monster (watch its red HP bar drop) and you build a *combo streak*.
5. *Wrong answer or running out of time:* the monster strikes the wizard and you *lose a heart* (you start with 2 hearts; lose both and the stage fails — you may retry).
6. Defeat the monster by correctly answering 6 words to clear the stage. You earn *1–3 stars*: 1 for clearing it, 2 for clearing with no hearts lost, 3 for a no-hearts-lost clear plus a 4+ answer combo.
7. Clear Stage 5 to rescue the princess and complete the game.

### Core Loop
*See Thai word → choose correct English word before time runs out → receive immediate visual/audio feedback → progress (damage monster / build combo) or setback (lose a heart) → repeat for 6 words → earn a star rating and move to the next stage.*

This loop repeats across 5 stages of increasing thematic variety, giving players a short, replayable feedback cycle (seconds per word) nested inside a longer progression goal (clearing all 5 stages).

### Feedback to the Player
- *Correctness:* answer buttons flash green (correct) or red (wrong) immediately on click.
- *Consequence feedback:* correct answers visibly damage the monster's HP bar; wrong/timeout answers trigger a screen shake, a red impact flash on the wizard, and a floating "-1" indicator so the cause of the heart loss is unambiguous.
- *Progress feedback:* a combo badge ("Combo x3!") appears for answer streaks; a time-bar glow warns when time is running low; a 1–3 star rating and confetti celebration appear on stage clear, giving players a clear sense of performance, not just pass/fail.
